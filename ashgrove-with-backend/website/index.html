Our story

Ashgrove began in 1998 as a three person renovation crew in Colombo. Homeowners kept asking us back, and then asking us to build. Today we deliver new homes, extensions and restorations across the western and central provinces.

We are a Colombo based building company. We build new homes, extend and renovate existing ones, and restore older houses. Every job has a written scope, a posted schedule and one person who is responsible for it.

Good to know

Questions we hear before every project.

How do I get an estimate?

Send the form below with a few lines about the property. We arrange a site visit, measure up and follow with a written estimate.

How soon can you start?

Start dates depend on drawings, permits and our current schedule. Typical lead time: 4 to 6 weeks.

Do you handle permits and engineering?

Yes. We prepare and submit local authority approval applications and coordinate structural, geotechnical and energy engineering so you have one point of contact.

How is pricing set?

You receive a written estimate that names every cost and the allowances for finishes. Changes after contract are priced in writing and approved by you before work starts.

Can you work on a heritage or character home?

Restoration is one of our three trades. We repair with matching materials and profiles wherever possible, and we work with heritage planners when a property is registered.

What happens if the schedule changes?

Weather, deliveries and inspections can move dates. Your supervisor updates the posted schedule and tells you in the weekly written update.

What warranty do you give?

Workmanship warranty: 5 years, plus the manufacturer warranties on every product we install.

Which areas do you serve?

We build and restore homes across Sri Lanka. Colombo, Gampaha, Kandy, Galle and Negombo
